Welcome to Refugio Madreselva

Where Nature Becomes Your Neighbour

Nestled high in the rainforest above Uvita, Refugio Madreselva is a private vacation home designed for those who seek genuine connection with nature. Whether you are a passionate birdwatcher, a wildlife photographer, or simply someone who craves peace and beauty, this is your sanctuary.


Each morning begins with the thunderous yet magical roar of howler monkeys echoing through the canopy. Sit by the infinity pool and watch clouds roll in from the Pacific, waves crash onto the iconic Whale's Tail peninsula, and a dazzling parade of birds cross the sky.

Wildlife & Nature

A Paradise for Birdwatchers

Nestled in the lush mountains above Uvita, Refugio Madreselva is a vibrant sanctuary for tropical birdlife. Our 24-acre private rainforest refuge offers a peaceful habitat for both resident and migratory species. Grab your binoculars and check out our official Refugio Madreselva eBird Hotspot to see recent sightings, track local species, and discover what’s flying through our canopy right now!

Fiery-billed Aracari

A small, colorful toucan found only on the Pacific slope of Costa Rica and western Panama. Look for its fiery orange bill as family groups move noisily through the canopy.

Golden-naped Woodpecker

A regional specialty with a bright golden nape and a bold white stripe down its black back. Pairs are often heard drumming and calling from tall dead trees.

Green Honeycreeper

Females are a vivid leaf green, while males glow iridescent blue-green with a black head; both have a bright yellow bill. Watch for them feeding on fruit and nectar with mixed flocks of tanagers.

Eye-ringed Flatbill

A quiet flycatcher of the shady understory, easily recognized by its bold white eye-ring and broad, flat bill. It often sits motionless before sallying out for insects.

White-crested Coquette

One of the smallest and most sought-after hummingbirds in the region, found only in southern Costa Rica and western Panama. The male sports a striking white crest.

White-whiskered Puffbird

Named for the white “whiskers” around its bill, this puffbird perches patiently in the forest understory, waiting to snatch large insects and small lizards.

Things To Do

Days Filled With Wonder

Whether you want pure relaxation or immersive nature experiences, Refugio Madreselva offers it all — right at your doorstep.

🦜

Birdwatching

Guided or self-guided birding walks through our private trail and surrounding primary forest. Bring your binoculars!

🥾

Forest Hiking

Our forested trail winds through the jungle to a serene waterfall — a perfect morning or afternoon escape.

🏊

Infinity Pool

Float in the pool while watching the clouds drift over the mountains and the Pacific shimmer far below.

📷

Wildlife Photography

Morpho butterflies, tree frogs, manakins, and hummingbirds — your lens will never run out of subjects.

🌊

Whale's Tail Beach

A short drive to the famous Whale's Tail sandbar in Marino Ballena National Park for snorkelling and whale watching.

🌅

Sunrise & Sunsets

Watch the sky turn gold and pink over the Pacific from the comfort of your hammock or pool deck.
